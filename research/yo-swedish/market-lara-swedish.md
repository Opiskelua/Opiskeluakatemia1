# Swedish YO abikurssi market: Lara and competitors

Researched 2026-10-30. [V] = verified on the cited page that day. [U] = unverified, from a secondary source or a search snippet.

## Summary (recommendation)

1. **Target keskipitkä ruotsi (RB / "B1-ruotsi", exam code BB) only.** Lara's only Swedish course is "Ruotsin abikurssi – keskipitkä oppimäärä". It has no pitkä (RA) product [V].
2. **The numbers back this up.** In 2026, 12,270 candidates registered for RB and 1,711 for RA (YTL). RB is about 88% of the Swedish second-language market, and RB autumn registrations (6,686) are higher than spring (5,584) [V].
3. **What Lara sells:** 59.99 € for Swedish alone, 99.99 € for English (long) + Swedish (save 20 €), valid until 1.4.2027. It covers all 4 exam sections, with vocabulary practice, explanatory feedback on every answer, essay training for both writing tasks, practice exams and spaced review. It publishes no counts of exercises or exams [V].
4. **Competitors:** WordDive (79 €, RA + RB, grade guarantee), Valmennuskeskus "Abinetti B-ruotsi" (125 €, RB-focused, 4 essays marked by a teacher), Otava "Fokus Abi" digital book (about 21 €), and Yle Abitreenit (free, both syllabi, past exams). Nobody has a dedicated online RA course.
5. **Advice:** build RB now. Price it at or below 59.99 € and offer an English + Swedish bundle. Cover all 4 sections plus the two writing tasks (200–350 and 400–600 characters). Treat RA as a later add-on at most, for example RA essay prompts only.

- These are registrations, not participants or grades. Lara quotes the same figure ("Keväällä 2026 kokeeseen ilmoittautui 5 584 kokelasta") [V].
- RB has a **bigger autumn sitting** than spring, unlike English. Many students sit Swedish in the autumn of their final year. Lara's licence covers only spring 2027, so the autumn RB sitting (next one 27.9.2027 per WordDive [V]) is a timing opportunity.
- The whole Swedish market is about a quarter the size of English long (about 14k vs 47k registrations a year).
- Spring 2026 grade cutoffs (L/E/M/C/B/A): RA 265/238/210/171/119/90; RB 248/214/177/139/111/90 [V] https://www.ylioppilastutkinto.fi/fi/tutkinnon-suorittaminen/pisterajat/pisterajat-kevat-2026

### RB exam format (for building the course)

- Max 299 points, up to 6 hours. Listening 80–90, reading 70–90, structures and vocabulary 20–40, writing 99. Two writing tasks: 200–350 and 400–600 characters, not counting spaces or line breaks. Per Lara's FAQ [V] https://lara.fi/ruotsin-abikurssi
- Short task 33 p, long task 66 p. An over-length answer loses 3 or 6 points. Long-syllabus writing is about 700–1,100 characters. From a school handout quoting YTL instructions (ouka.fi), which I did not open [U] https://ouka.fi/media/11277/download
- Exam dates: spring 2027 RB/RA on Wed 31.3.2027 [V Lara]. Registration deadline 1.12.2026 [V Lara]. Autumn 2027 on 27.9.2027 [V WordDive].
