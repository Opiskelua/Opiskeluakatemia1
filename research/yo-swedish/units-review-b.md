# Review B: Swedish (RB) Polku units sv-substantiv, sv-adjektiv, sv-pronomen

Scope: every item, guide (rule, examples, reminder) and explanation in `opiskeluakatemia-content/src/data/learning-path-sv/`
`unit-substantiv.ts`, `unit-adjektiv.ts`, `unit-pronomen.ts` (6 + 7 + 6 lessons/checkpoints, 146 items).
Grading checked against `src/lib/learning-path-v2/grading.ts` (`normalizeText`: case, whitespace and `.,!?;:"` ignored; hyphens and å/ä/ö significant; order answers must equal an `accepted` sentence).

Method: I solved every item blind from the key-free sheets first (all options I'd call grammatical and natural, all gap answers I'd accept, all grammatical orders of the tokens), then compared with the unit files. My blind answers matched every choice key and every gap `accepted` list. For order items, every grammatical order I found is in `accepted`, with no missing variants. I found **no S1 issues**. There is one S2: a second defensible option in pronomen-2-q2. Everything else is S3 (a guide rule contradicted by an item, explanation overgeneralisations, a false grammatical claim, Finnish polish, and a near-copy checkpoint item).

Severity: S1 = wrong key / correct answer marked wrong / wrong rule; S2 = second defensible option or missing accepted variant (including an accepted answer that is wrong); S3 = wording/explanation polish.

## Checked and fine (notable borderline cases considered)

- **Flagged fronted-place orders** (sv-substantiv-3-q8 `i staden finns det många museer`, 5-q7 `i Stockholm bor min systers pojkvän`, sv-adjektiv-4-q8 `i Sverige bor min bästa vän`): all three are grammatical V2 sentences, natural with contrastive or topic focus, and mean the same as the Finnish prompt. Accepting them is correct. I found no further grammatical orders of the tokens in any of them.
- **sv-substantiv-4-q5 (kycklingar)**: *åt vi kycklingar och ris* would mean several whole birds (or chicks), and *kycklingen* needs a previously mentioned bird. Neither is defensible as "we had chicken for dinner". The key *kyckling* is the only natural option, and the explanation is accurate.
- **sv-adjektiv-4-q6 (Största delen)**: *Största delen av eleverna har…* is standard. *De flesta eleverna* is more common with countables, but it is not an option, and *största delen av* + plural count noun is accepted usage. *Den största delen* is also grammatical but not offered. The other two options are clearly wrong. Fine.
- **sv-substantiv-1-q2 (söker ett jobb)**: this is grammatical, so it is not a key or grading problem. I listed it above only as an optional S3 naturalness improvement.
- **Flagged fronted-object orders**: sv-pronomen-2-q8 `sin gamla cykel sålde han` is a marked but grammatical topicalisation. A reflexive possessive in a fronted object coreferent with the subject is acceptable (cf. *Sin mor har han inte träffat på länge*), and accepting it does no harm. sv-pronomen-3-q8 `det här huset tycker jag om` is grammatical and natural (contrastive), with the particle stranded correctly. Both are fine.
- **sv-pronomen-3-q6 (det/detta)**: *Det hade jag aldrig trott* is the natural form. *Detta hade jag aldrig trott* is more formal but correct. Both are accepted. Fine.
- **Possessive items with the Finnish meaning in the instruction** (sv-pronomen-2-q1, 2-q2, 2-q5, cp-q2, 5-q4, 5-q5): the glosses are necessary, because without them *hennes/deras/dem* would be defensible (another person's mother/puppy/anniversary; helping or dressing other people). With the glosses, each item has exactly one correct option. The exception is 2-q2, because *talonsa* is ambiguous for number (S2 above). In 2-q5, *uuden koiranpentunsa kanssa* is unambiguously singular (genitive *uuden*), so *sin* is the only answer. In 5-q4 the gloss gives *toisiaan* → *varandra* fairly directly. That is acceptable, because the point tested is the distinction from *sig/dem*.
- sv-pronomen-2-q3, 2-q6, 2-q7: these have no gloss, but none is needed. In 2-q3 the subject is *jag*. The 2-q6 hint restricts the answer to sin-forms. In 2-q7, *hennes* is the answer under both readings (sin cannot be in the att-clause subject).
- sv-pronomen-1-q5 *mycket läxor*: colloquially very common (*läxor* treated as a mass). Fine at RB.
- sv-pronomen-5-q2: bare *som* without a stranded preposition is ungrammatical, so *där* is unique. sv-pronomen-5-q7: *vilket* is wrong (an en-noun would need *vilken*), so the key is unique.
- sv-pronomen-cp-q8 *känner varandra bra*: *väl* is a little more formal and idiomatic, but *bra* is fine and is the only token.
- sv-pronomen-cp-q6 has the same frame and options as 5-q1 (vilket/som/vad), but the sentence is different. That is acceptable for a checkpoint.
- sv-substantiv-2-q5 *spelar gitarr*: correct, and the explanation is accurate. sv-substantiv-2-q7 / adjektiv-2-q7 / substantiv-1-q7 / adjektiv-1-q7 / 5-q8 / cp-q8: all grammatical orders are accepted. Mid-field time adverbials such as *jag har i dag ont i huvudet* are marginal at best and rightly not accepted.
- sv-substantiv-4-q8 / sv-adjektiv-5-q8: all three positions of *numera/nu* are accepted. That is correct.
- sv-adjektiv-2 reminder (*Maten smakar gott*): correct, because *gott* is adverbial with *smaka*. sv-adjektiv-2-q5 *gatan ser tom ut*: correct.
- sv-adjektiv-6-q6 *mitt är minst*: correct predicative superlative, and *minsta* is not possible here. 6-q5 *desto*: *så* is not acceptable in standard Swedish.
- Guide rules checked against their own items: the substantiv-1/2/4/5, adjektiv-1/2/3/5/6 and pronomen-1/2/3/4/5 rules hold for every item in their lessons, apart from the two noted above (substantiv-3 museum, adjektiv-4 *den förra*). The 4-q5 option *det förra året* is correctly treated as wrong as a time adverbial.
- Finnish-language claims checked: pronomen-2 rule (*äidilleen* vs *hänen äidilleen*) is accurate, and pronomen-1 reminder (he/ne → de, heidät/heitä/heille → dem) is accurate.
- Level: *bokrean, besvara, hundvalp, planeringen, samhälle, arbetsplats* are all within reach at RB/B1, and nothing is far above level.
