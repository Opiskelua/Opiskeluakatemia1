# Finnish matriculation exam: Swedish as second national language, medium syllabus (RB) and long syllabus (RA): format, grading, grade boundaries, HVPs

Researched 2026-10-30, using the same method as `research/yo-english/ytl-exam-format-hvp.md`. Primary sources are YTL's own pages, the exam regulations (määräykset) and the 20 HVP ("hyvän vastauksen piirteet") documents for BB/BA from K2022 to S2026. I downloaded and parsed all of them; nothing here comes from summaries.
Legend: **[V]** means verified directly from the cited official source. **[D]** means derived by me from official documents (for example, summed from HVP tables). **[U]** means unverified or secondary.

**Codes [V]:** in YTL file names, Swedish medium syllabus (keskipitkä, B1 syllabus, the "RB" exam) is **`BB`**, and Swedish long syllabus (pitkä, A syllabus, the "RA" exam) is **`BA`**. Both exams are held on the same day. The HVPs are written in Swedish (section names "Del 1: Hörförståelse" and so on), with grading notes in Finnish.

## 1. Exam structure

### 1.1 What applies to both RB and RA [V]
- **Two levels** (MÄÄR §1.2):
  - The long syllabus (pitkä) exam is based on the **A syllabus** of the 2019 upper-secondary core curriculum (LOPS 2019) and counts as the "vaativampi koe" (more demanding exam) under law 502/2019 §3.
  - The medium syllabus (keskipitkä) exam is based on the **B1 syllabus**.
  - Candidates choose either level, whatever they studied at school.
  - A bilingual candidate may replace the second-national-language exam with the mother-tongue exam in Swedish (MÄÄR §1.2).
- **Target CEFR levels** (MÄÄR §2 table, LOPS 2019):

| Syllabus | Interaction | Interpreting texts | Producing texts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swedish A (RA) | B2.1 | B2.1 | B1.2 |
| Swedish B1 (RB) | B1.1 | B1.1 | B1.1 |

- **Four skill areas** (MÄÄR §3, AINE): kuullun ymmärtäminen (listening comprehension), luetun ymmärtäminen (reading comprehension), kirjallinen tuottaminen (writing) and sanaston ja rakenteiden hallinta (vocabulary and structures). Swedish includes listening (MÄÄR table 2).
  - To pass, the candidate must leave answers in the listening task set **and** in at least one other skill area (MÄÄR §3).
- **Maximum 299 points**, with these bands (MÄÄR §5; AINE). They are the same for RB, RA and English:

| Section (YTL term / HVP heading) | Points |
|---|---|
| Kuullun ymmärtäminen / Del 1: Hörförståelse | **80–90** |
| Luetun ymmärtäminen / Del 2: Läsförståelse | **70–90** |
| Sanasto ja rakenteet / Del 3: Ordförråd och strukturer | **20–40** |
| Kirjallinen tuottaminen / Del 4: Skriftlig framställning | **99** |
| **Total** | **299** |

- **Time and conditions** (MÄÄR §4):
  - The exam lasts **at most 6 hours** and takes place on one day.
  - Candidates choose the task order and can change answers until they end the exam.
  - They can return to the written materials and to *some* listening materials.
  - The exam runs in a closed network with **no dictionaries**. Headphones are required.
  - Drafting on paper is allowed, but drafts are not graded.
- The YLEISET details (start no earlier than 09:00, no leaving before 12:00) are as recorded in the English research doc. I did not re-check them here **[U]**.
- Task types are **not announced in advance**, and spring and autumn exams may differ in form. The regulations name "erilaiset monivalinta- ja aukkotehtävät sekä vapaat tuottamistehtävät" (various multiple-choice and gap-fill tasks, and free production tasks) (MÄÄR §1.4, §3).
- Listening: some texts can be heard several times and some only once, and some may be videos (MÄÄR §5.3).

### 1.2 The difference between RB and RA is only in writing [V]
- **RB:** two writing tasks. One **short task (33 p, 200–350 characters)** and one **longer task (66 p, 400–600 characters)**, each with **2 alternative prompts**. Prompts are given in the school's language (Finnish). The candidate does one from each pair (MÄÄR §5.3.1.2).
- **RA:** **one** long text, **99 p, 700–1,100 characters**, chosen from several prompts. Every RA exam from K2022 to S2026 offered **4 prompts**, and the prompts are written in Swedish (MÄÄR §5.3.1.1; HVPs).
- Characters exclude spaces and line breaks. The exam system counts characters but does not stop the candidate going over (MÄÄR §5.3).

Observations **[D]**:
- **Swedish is much more stable than English.** From K2022 to S2025, the standard layout was **90 / 75 / 35 / 99** in both RB and RA. Vocabulary and structures was nearly always **15 + 5 + 15** (section 2).
- **Trend since 2026:** vocabulary and structures shrank (35 → 32 → 29) and reading grew (75 → 78 → 80/81). The translation or dialogue task went from 15 p to 12 p (K2026) to 9 p (S2026).
- **Out-of-band listening totals in RA:** S2023 had 92 and S2026 (preliminary HVP) has 91, both above the 80–90 band. I checked S2026 item by item: 15+15+12+15+12+9+13 = 91. Either the band is not applied strictly, or the final HVP will change. Treat this as a curiosity, not a rule.

## 2. Task types by section (from BB/BA HVPs 2022–2026) [V unless marked]

General rules printed at the top of the HVPs (from S2022 onward; the K2022 HVP has only the first sentence):
- "Täydet pisteet voi saada vain vastaus, joka ei sisällä väärää tietoa." (Only an answer that contains no wrong information can get full points.)
- "Jos avoimen kysymyksen vastauksen pituudelle on annettu merkkimäärärajoitus, seuraa sen ylittämisestä pistevähennys. **Vähennys on 1 piste** riippumatta kysymyksen maksimipisteistä." (Going over an open answer's character limit costs **1 point**, whatever the item's maximum.)

### Del 1: Hörförståelse (listening), 6–7 tasks, about 90 p
- Multiple-choice questions in Swedish, usually **3 p** per item. A "best title" item is often 2 p (for example K2026 BB 1.5).
- **Open answers in Finnish** ("Vastaa suomeksi"), usually **3 p, all or nothing**. Two-point answers such as "(2 asiaa)" are worth 4 p.
  - Each HVP lists accepted answers plus "Nollainfoa" (zero-value information) that neither gains nor loses points.
  - The character cap was **150** (2022 to S2023), then **100** from K2024, with some items capped at **50**.
  - RB has about 8–12 Finnish-answer items in the whole exam and RA about 8–12 (count of "suomeksi" in each HVP).
- One to three **video tasks** per exam: "Du kan titta på videon så många gånger du vill" (you can watch the video as many times as you like).
- Audio is heard "först i sin helhet och sedan i delar" (first in full, then in parts) or "två gånger" (twice).
- Recurring formats: news broadcasts ("Nyheter"), dialogues, public announcements ("Viktiga meddelanden till allmänheten", where you pick why the message was sent), interviews and radio features.

### Del 2: Läsförståelse (reading), 6–7 tasks, 75–81 p
- Multiple choice, Finnish open answers (3/0 p), matching (for example "Välj rätt rubrik för recepten", choose the right heading for each recipe), "välj det påstående som bäst passar in på varje text" (choose the statement that best fits each text), news items, ads and blog texts.
- Topics are often Finland-Swedish ("Inspirerande berättelser från Svenskfinland", "Uleåborg – Europas kulturhuvudstad 2026").

### Del 3: Ordförråd och strukturer (vocabulary and structures), always 3 tasks
These are the recurring task types, quoting the HVP instructions.

1. **Long multiple-choice cloze, 15 gaps × 1 p = 15 p.** It appears in every BB and BA exam from K2022 to S2026. Instruction: "Läs texten och välj på varje punkt det alternativ som bäst passar in i sammanhanget" (read the text and choose the alternative that best fits the context at each gap).
   - The gaps test prepositions (*av*, *på*), conjunctions (*för att*, *utan*), word order ("inte alltid finns"), verb forms (*har blivit*, *serveras*), definite and plural noun forms (*orsakerna*, *befolkningen*), adverbs (*stadigt*, *Samtidigt*) and fixed phrases (*till och med*).
   - K2022 BA was the one exception: an open-answer item ("om").
2. **Short task, 5 gaps × 1 p = 5 p.** Its format rotates:
   - K2022 BB/BA "Motsatser": give the opposite word in the right form (productive), for example *stängt* → *öppnat / har öppnat*, *tillåtet* → *förbjudet*.
   - S2022 BB "Vad säger du?": pick the best reply to a situation (multiple choice).
   - K2023 BB, S2023 BB/BA: **word bank** with 3–5 extra words ("Det blir fem ord över", five words are left over). K2024 BB "fyll i med de ord som bäst passar" (fill in the words that fit best) is probably the same format **[U]**.
   - **Productive gaps with a Finnish cue** (S2024 BB, K2025 BB/BA, K2024 BA, S2024 BA, S2025 BA, K2026 BA, S2026 BA). Instructions such as "Läs texten och översätt de finska uttrycken så att de passar in i sammanhanget" (translate the Finnish expressions so that they fit the context) and "fyll i luckorna … med hjälp av tips på finska" (fill in the gaps using the Finnish hints). Examples:
     - RB: *on kasvanut* → *har växt*; *puolet* → *hälften av*; *viimeisen vuoden aikana* → *(under) det senaste året*; *He oppivat* → *De lär sig*; *on asunut* → *har bott*; *tänne* → *hit*.
     - RA: *kahdenkymmenen vuoden ajan* → *i tjugo års tid / i tjugo år / under tjugo år*; *korkealaatuisia* → *av hög kvalitet*; *lainsäädännössä* → *i lagstiftningen*.
     - Followed by "Myös muut oikeat ilmaisut hyväksytään" (other correct expressions are also accepted).
   - **Phrase multiple choice** (S2025, K2026, S2026 BB): "välj det alternativ som passar bäst" (choose the alternative that fits best), with phrase-level options such as *sin första egna bostad*.
   - K2023 BA: dialogue multiple choice. K2024 BA: "Översätt och böj orden" (translate and inflect the words).
   - S2022 BA was the one deviation: a 10 p word bank with 5 extra words.
3. **Dialogue translation, Finnish to Swedish**, with a 3 p line split into **1 p per chunk**. Instruction: "Läs först dialogen och översätt sedan X:s repliker till svenska" (read the dialogue first, then translate X's lines into Swedish).
   - **5 lines × 3 p = 15 p** from K2022 to S2025, **4 × 3 = 12 p** in K2026 and **3 × 3 = 9 p** in S2026 (RA S2026: "till naturlig svenska", into natural Swedish).
   - The HVP lists the accepted variants for each chunk, for example:
     - "Kuulostaa kivalta. Mitä elokuvaa olet ajatellut?" → "(Det) låter kul/roligt/bra… (1 p.) Vilken film (1 p.) har du tänkt (dig/på) / funderat (på) (1 p.)?"
     - Some words are marked as required ("kan vaaditaan", "just vaaditaan", meaning *kan* and *just* must be there).
   - Topics are everyday ones: booking cinema tickets, a boss calling about extra shifts, a product complaint ("Reklamation"), an invitation, a gift purchase.
   - The task is partly graded centrally by YTL. The K2026 BB item 15.4 grading was corrected after appeals: +1 p for 17 candidates (YTL news 15.6.2026, https://www.ylioppilastutkinto.fi/fi/ajankohtaista/kevaan-kokeiden-arvostelussa-muutoksia).
4. **New in S2026 BB: guided dialogue production** ("I studentköket", in the student kitchen, 3 × 3 p = 9 p), replacing translation in RB.
   - The candidate writes their own lines from **Finnish instructions in brackets**, for example "(Kerro, mistä ruokaohje on peräisin.)" (say where the recipe comes from). "Använd hela meningar. Svaret ska vara 5–10 ord långt." (Use whole sentences. The answer should be 5–10 words long.)
   - Rubric 3/2/1/0. 3 p means fully appropriate with flawless content and language. 2 p means one weakness (content, language or appropriateness). 1 p means several weaknesses. A **2–4 word answer gets at most 1 p** and a **1-word answer gets 0 p**.
   - This is from the preliminary HVP. It is the first time the format appears in BB or BA 2022–2026 **[D]**.

## 3. Writing

### 3.1 RB (keskipitkä): 2 tasks, 33 + 66 = 99 p [V, MÄÄR §5.3.1.2, §5.3.2.2, §6.2; HVPs]
- **Kort skrivuppgift (short writing task): 33 p, 200–350 characters.** **Längre skrivuppgift (longer writing task): 66 p, 400–600 characters.** Each has **2 prompts** and the candidate answers one of each, **in Swedish**.
  - The prompts are in Finnish, though a prompt may include a Swedish message or reader's letter to respond to.
  - Every BB HVP from S2022 to S2026 prints "Kirjoitussuorituksen pituus: 200–350 merkkiä / 400–600 merkkiä" (length of the written answer: 200–350 / 400–600 characters) [V].
- Prompt types seen:
  - Short: an online ad for a roommate, a consoling message, an encouraging social-media message, a WhatsApp message, a competition entry, tips for a reader page.
  - Long: an opinion piece for an online paper's letters page (school food), a made-up news story with a headline, a writing-contest entry ("En dag som Finlands president", a day as Finland's president), a post for an online discussion.
- **Over the limit:** **−3 p** on a 33-p task and **−6 p** on a 66-p task, applied from the first character over. **Under the minimum:** no separate deduction, but the answer "ei … voi yltää korkeimpiin pisteisiin" (cannot reach the top scores).
- If a candidate answers both prompts of a pair, the **lowest-scoring** answers count.
- Copying sentences straight from the exam texts lowers the score. Using words and structures from the material is allowed.
- **Criteria** (overall judgement driven mainly by communicativeness):
  - *Viestinnällisyys* (communicativeness): "Mitä kokelas on halunnut ilmaista, ja kuinka hän on ilmaisussaan onnistunut?" (What did the candidate want to express, and how well did they succeed?)
  - *Tehtävänannon noudattaminen ja aiheen käsittely* (following the prompt and treating the topic)
  - *Kielellinen laajuus ja tarkkuus* (range and accuracy of language)
- **Allowed scores (only these):**

| Band | Short (33) | Long (66) | Communicativeness descriptor (summary) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 33 / 31 / 29 | 66 / 62 / 58 | clear, natural, fluent; easy to read; answers the prompt fully; wide range, very good control |
| 2 | 27 / 25 | 54 / 50 | fairly clear and relatively natural; answers well; ordinary treatment; some errors |
| 3 | 23 / 21 | 46 / 42 | satisfactory; hard to read in places; mostly answers; fairly narrow; several errors |
| 4 | 19 / 17 | 38 / 34 | unclear; partly answers; limited range; many errors even in basic structures |
| 5 | 15 / 13 | 30 / 26 | weak, unclear in places; deficient |
| 6 | 11 / 9 | 22 / 18 | barely gets across |
| 7 | 7 / 5 / 3 / 0 | 14 / 10 / 6 / 0 | does not get across / did not do the task |

- In the regulations in force for K2024 to K2025 (approved 7.11.2023, Wayback snapshot of 19.3.2025), the medium-syllabus scale and deductions are identical to today's. I found no record of when this scale was introduced **[U]**. The autumn-2023 overhaul to 10 scores covered only the long syllabus.
- HVP extras: some prompt notes cap the score, for example K2022 BB "jos tilaston tulkinnassa on virheitä … ei suoritus voi olla ylintä kategoriaa" (if the statistics are misread, the answer cannot be in the top category).

### 3.2 RA (pitkä): 1 task, 99 p [V, MÄÄR §5.3.1.1, §5.3.2.1, §6.1; HVPs]
- **700–1,100 characters.** Swedish is not one of the 700–1,300 exceptions; those are only long English and long Finnish.
- The candidate picks **1 of 4 prompts**. HVP wording: "Skriv en text på 700–1 100 tecken … Om du skriver färre än 700 tecken är det mycket svårt att uppfylla uppgiftskraven." (Write a text of 700–1,100 characters. If you write fewer than 700, it is very difficult to meet the task requirements.)
- Example prompts (S2026): a letter to a friend, a reflective piece based on the material, an argumentative forum post and a forum post for young people.
- **Over 1,100 characters: −9 p**, whatever the length of the overrun. Under 700: no deduction, but the answer cannot reach the top scores. Small omissions (title, letter conventions): a deduction under 10 p. Mostly copied from the material: 40–0 p. Plagiarised or entirely off-topic: 0 p. If more than one prompt is answered, the lowest score counts.
- **Scale, same as long English:** **99, 94, 84, 74, 64, 54, 44, 28, 14, 0**, across five criteria: communicativeness; content and treatment of the topic; structure that fits the text type; range of language; correctness and seriousness of errors. Introduced autumn 2023 (see the English doc §3 for the descriptors and history; MÄÄR §6.1 is the same text).
- Benchmark essays ("maamerkit") exist for second-national-language exams. They are linked from the YTL blog: https://www.ylioppilastutkinto.fi/fi/ajankohtaista/blogi/maamerkkeja-arvostelun-tueksi-toisen-kotimaisen-kielen-ja-joidenkin-vieraiden. I did not open them.

## 5. Exam dates [V]

## 6. Regulation changes from autumn 2025 [V/D]

- **Current regulations:** approved 4.6.2025 and first applied to the **autumn 2025** exam.
- **Previous version:** approved 7.11.2023 and applied from spring 2024. I compared it with today's text, using the Wayback snapshot of 19.3.2025.
- **For Swedish the changes are editorial, not structural** [D, diff]:
  - References to LOPS 2015 were removed; the regulations now refer only to LOPS 2019. The transition clause "Keväästä 2023 kevääseen 2025 asti … otetaan huomioon sekä LOPS 2015 että 2019" (from spring 2023 to spring 2025, both curricula are taken into account) is gone.
  - Course-based wording was changed to credits (opintopisteet).
  - Latin's extended short syllabus exam was dropped.
- **Unchanged:** the 299 maximum, the section bands, the writing lengths (RB 200–350 / 400–600, RA 700–1,100), the deductions (−3 / −6 / −9) and both criteria tables.
- **What actually changed in the exams after autumn 2025 comes from the HVPs, not the regulations [D]:**
  - Vocabulary and structures shrank (35 → 32 → 29) and reading grew (K2026, S2026).
  - The translation task shrank (15 → 12 → 9 p).
  - S2026 BB replaced translation with guided dialogue production (5–10 words per line, 3/2/1/0 rubric).
  - All of this fits within MÄÄR's "task types are not announced in advance" rule.
- **Planned, not decided [V]:**
  - An **oral component** for long and medium syllabus language exams, **including second national language**, to be introduced in stages. Exam length and the 299 maximum stay the same, and the oral share is "pieni" (small).
  - It is planned as a separate part on the same day in the same room, done individually in the exam system, where candidates can listen to and re-record their answer.
  - A report is due by the end of 2026 and a consultation in early 2027. No schedule has been decided. Page updated 2.10.2026: https://www.ylioppilastutkinto.fi/fi/tutkinnon-toimeenpano/suullinen-kielitaito-ylioppilaskokeissa
- **No Swedish equivalent of the English 2028 "all in target language" decision.** I found nothing announced that would end the Finnish-answer items or Finnish-cue tasks in the Swedish exams [V, AINE news list shows only the English decision].
- **K2026 grading correction** (15.6.2026): BB task 15.4 (translation, partly graded centrally) was fixed: +1 p for 2 appellants and 15 others.

## 8. Conflicts and cautions

1. **The RA writing limit is 700–1,100, not 1,300.** Only long English and long Finnish use 1,300. Don't copy the EA numbers.
2. **RB writing deductions are −3 / −6, not −9**, and each task has its own maximum (33 and 66). A candidate who overruns both loses 9 p in total.
3. **The open-answer overrun deduction** is a flat 1 p in every Swedish HVP from S2022 on. MÄÄR says "proportional, at least 1 p" and defers to the HVP.
4. **Listening above 90 in RA** (S2023: 92; S2026 preliminary: 91) contradicts the 80–90 band. Don't build mock exams that rely on the bands being exact.
5. **Vocabulary and structures is shrinking in 2026** (35 → 29), and the S2026 RB translation was replaced with guided dialogue writing. Course content that drills only 5-line translation may be out of date. Cover both formats.
6. **The K2027 date (Wednesday 31.3.2027) breaks the usual Monday pattern.** The dates are provisional per YTL.
7. **Not opened or verified:** the maamerkit benchmark essays for Swedish; the HVPs from 2021 and earlier; the Abitreenit contents and the S2024 Abitreenit path; the old (pre-2023) regulations PDF (downloaded, but no PDF text tool was available); the YLEISET start and leave times (taken from the English doc, not re-checked).
