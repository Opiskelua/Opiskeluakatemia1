# Student flashcard decks for RB Swedish (keskipitkä ruotsi): vocabulary frequency vs. our course decks

*Compiled 2026-11-01. A frequency signal only. No student card is to be imported; every card in our course is written by us. The Finnish glosses below are our own, not copied from the decks.*

Legend: **[V]** = term seen in a deck page that we actually read (via a page-to-text fetch of the public Brainscape/Knowt page). **[U]** = term known only from a search-result snippet (the page itself could not be opened). Every term in the ranked table (section 3) and the missing list (section 4) is [V]. The Quizlet material in section 2.2 is [U].

## 1. Bottom line

- **Quizlet blocked every fetch.** All URL forms we tried returned HTTP 403: `quizlet.com/fi/<id>/<slug>/`, `quizlet.com/<id>/<slug>/` and `quizlet.com/<id>/`. We did not try to get around the bot block. We catalogued 33 relevant Quizlet sets from search results (section 2.2), but we only know their titles and a few snippet terms [U].
- **What we actually read were 61 decks from 16 independent authors**: 60 on Brainscape and 1 on Knowt. They hold **4,900 cards** and give 3,576 distinct normalised lemmas. About 40 decks are explicitly ABI, YO or lukio theme decks. The rest are lukio course decks (RUB1–7, Fokus 4/7, "ruotsi 5") or YO-titled phrase and verb decks.
- **311 terms** turn up with at least 3 authors, or with 2 authors and at least 3 decks. Our 585 cards fully cover **97** of them, cover **30** only partially (the word appears only inside one of our phrases or in a note), and miss **184**.
- **The biggest gaps** come in five groups:
  - **Adverbs and connectors.** We have no deck for these at all: *egentligen, ofta, ganska, dessutom, redan, ännu, snart, kanske, nästan, till och med, ändå, samtidigt, sällan, alltid, ibland, fastän, även om, däremot, alltså, troligen, faktiskt, fortfarande, tyvärr …*. The exam catalogue (§6) independently lists these same adverbs as recurring MC keys.
  - **Housing (boende).** This is a whole exam theme with no deck: *bostad, bo, studentbostad, hyreslägenhet, etta/tvåa, vardagsrum, möbel, omgivning, höghus/radhus/villa …*.
  - **Diversity and multicultural everyday life (mångfald).** Examples: *diskriminera/diskriminering, acceptera, jämlik/jämlikhet, främlingsfientlighet, minoritet/majoritet, integration, fördom, kulturskillnad, anpassa sig*.
  - **Post-gymnasium studies.** Examples: *ta studenten, avlägga examen, inträdesprov/urvalsprov, huvudämne/biämne, studielån, studiestöd, studiepoäng, föreläsning, tenta, termin, ta sabbatsår/mellanår*.
  - **Traditions, values and religion.** Examples: *jul, påsk, midsommar, nyår, fira, bastu, religion, religiös, kyrka, livsåskådning, värdering*.
- Smaller gaps are personality adjectives (*ärlig, artig, ansvarsfull, hjälpsam, blyg*), health phrases (*bli sjuk, bli förkyld, ha ont i halsen, må illa, orka*), traffic (*cykla, körkort, fotgängare, övergångsställe*), money (*inkomst, lån, kosta, dyr*) and food (*frukost, middag, mellanmål, smaka*).
- **How reliable the student decks are:** a page-reading pass flagged errors on **~6.7 %** of the cards it read (329 of 4,900). This is a lower bound, because the reader under-detects errors. Rates range from **0–2.5 %** for decks transcribed from a textbook list (Peltoniemi 1.1 %, Mäntylä 2.4 %) to **15–17 %** for self-typed decks (Tähtinen 16.7 %, Parkkari 15.1 %). The errors are mostly typos (*utblida, stjeker, sädar*), wrong en/ett (*öppna universitet, en tyg, en veckopengar*), wrong inflection (*skrämt* as a form of *vara rädd*, *fötts* for *fött*) and wrong glosses (*fast* = "siksi että", *leva sambo* = "elää avioliitossa", *tuhma* → *dum*). Use the decks as a frequency signal only. Never copy them.

Brainscape pages were read through a page-to-text model, which was asked to list only the Swedish side of each card as normalised lemmas and to flag obvious errors. So a count of "3 authors" sometimes means 2 textbooks plus 1 independent student. That is still a real signal of what students drill, but it is biased towards those books' theme lists, especially housing and diversity.

**What the snippets add [U].** Some snippet-only phrases are missing from our decks, or present only in part:

- **Opinions:** *jag tycker att …* (we only have *tycka om* and *tycka synd om*), *jag tror att …*, *det stämmer / det stämmer inte*, *du har rätt / fel*, *min syn på saken är att …*.

- **Letter closings:** *Hör av dig!* We have *höra av sig* only as a verb card. *Sköt om dig!* is not in our decks.
- **Time expressions:** *i går, i morgon, i förrgår, i övermorgon*.

## 3. Ranked list (top 311)

Ranking: first by the number of **independent authors** whose decks contain the lemma, then by the number of decks. Authors count first so that one person's 14 theme decks don't dominate. Function words (pronouns, prepositions, *och, inte, vara, ha, kan, ska …*) were dropped, because several decks were split into single words by the reader. Lemmas are normalised: lowercased, with en/ett/att, *ngt/ngn* and parenthesised forms stripped, and slash variants split. Gloss = our own most common Finnish equivalent. "In our decks" means: **yes** = has its own card, **partial** = appears only inside one of our phrases or notes, **no** = absent.

## 5. Our own cards: things that look off (flag only)

No clear Swedish or Finnish errors were found in the 585 cards. Minor points:

- **Convention breaks (uncountable nouns).** The file header says uncountable nouns get no article and show the definite form. Four cards break that: *en utveckling* (TEKNIK-11), *en framtid* (ARBETE-02), *en vänskap* (FAMILJ-12) and *en kärlek* (FAMILJ-13). By our own convention they should read *utveckling (utvecklingen)*, *framtid (framtiden)* and so on.
- **Rare supines.** *ska (skall) – skulle – skolat* (VERB-65) and *måste – måste – måst* (VERB-67): *skolat* and *måst* exist but are rare. Consider adding "(harvinainen)" or dropping the supine.
- **Duplicate card.** *beroende av* (TEKNIK-19) duplicates *vara beroende av* (PHRASAL-64). That is harmless, but the themes.ts header says identical chunks should not be repeated.
- **Missing core opinion verb.** *tycka* in the sense "olla mieltä" (*jag tycker att …*) has no card of its own. It is the opinion verb students drill most often (decks plus Quizlet snippets). Only *tycka om* and *tycka synd om* exist.

Typical faults:

- **Typos.** *utblida, intråfesprovet, stjeker, sädar, hälsonkunskap, lagstadiet, görä*.
- **Wrong gender.** *öppna universitet* for *öppet*, *en tyg*, *en veckopengar*, *en tradion*, *ett köpare*.
- **Wrong form.** *fötts* for *fött*, *hålt* for *hållit*, *följar* for *följer*, *immigranten* as a plural, *sova för liten*.
- **Wrong gloss.** *fast* = "siksi että" (it means although), *leva sambo* = "elää avioliitossa", *tuhma* → *dum*, *meri* → *sjö*, *geologia* → *geografi*, *Mitä ihmettä?* → *Vad säger du?*, *Eipä kestä* paired with *Tack för hjälpen*.
- **Questionable usage.** *sådeles* for "näin ollen", and *påverka till*.

**Verdict:** decks transcribed from a textbook list are near-clean. Self-typed decks run at 10–17 %, which is roughly one card in six to ten. Treat all student decks as **frequency evidence only**. Never import their wording, genders or glosses.

## 7. Method notes and limitations

- **No Quizlet terms in the counts.** Quizlet is the most-used host but returned 403, so the counts rest on Brainscape plus one Knowt deck. The Quizlet titles we found match the same Otava *Abi ruotsi* and Fokus chapter structure, so the theme mix is probably similar.
- **Counts are by lemma.** A theme word that is phrased differently in different decks (e.g. *studentbostad* vs. *studentlya*) is counted separately. Several decks split phrase cards into single words, which inflates very basic verbs.
- **Curriculum bias.** Words that RB tests often but students rarely put on cards are under-represented here, because these lists mirror textbook theme vocabulary and not the exam's MC keys. Examples: *öka, minska, bidra, behov, efterfrågan, utmaning*. Cross-check with `past-exams-grammar-catalogue.md` §6 (which our decks already partly cover: *öka, minska, påverka, bidra till, behov, utmaning, framgång, möjlighet, orsak*).
- **Intermediate files** are in the session scratchpad (merge script, per-deck lemma lists). They are not committed.
