# What Finnish abi teachers actually teach for RB (keskipitkä ruotsi): peda.net and open teacher materials

Researched 2026-11-01. Companion to `past-exams-grammar-catalogue.md` (what the exam tests) and `abitreenit-rb-guidance.md` (Yle Abitreenit; not repeated here). Purpose: see what upper-secondary Swedish teachers emphasise in abi and revision courses, which rules and mnemonics students already know, which phrase lists they get, and where teacher material is wrong or oversimplified, so our Ruotsin abikurssi neither contradicts what students learned nor copies errors.

**Markers.** **[V]** = verified: I read the page or attached file that day. **[U]** = unverified: seen only as a search-result snippet (the page was gone, locked or not fetched). **[I]** = my inference or judgement.

Copyright note: teacher pages and the Otava guide are summarised, and only short phrases are quoted. Phrase lists are standard situational Swedish. We should write our own wording, not copy theirs.

### 2.2 Order teachers use [V]
- **Polvijärvi (S1)** gives the "should master" list in this order: noun inflection and definiteness → adjective inflection (predicative, attributive, adverb) → comparison → pronouns → verb inflection and tenses (+ strong verbs) → future, imperative, conditional, modals, *att* → (s-passive) → (present participle) → main and subordinate word order → time expressions. Bracketed items are optional, so this teacher treats passive and participles as lower priority for RB.
- **Forssa RUB 20 (S10)** pairs one theme with one grammar point per lesson: time and place prepositions → pronouns → comparison → indirect question → irregular verbs and derived verbs → infinitive → conditional → noun use → conjunctions and word order → s-passive and s-verbs → present participle.
- **Polvijärvi abikurssi (S3)** spends four lessons on preposition expressions (adjectives, verbs A–L, verbs L–Ä, nouns, then "others"), alternating with old-exam sections and weekly listening.
- **Töölö RUB8 (S11)**: lesson 2 covers verb inflection and tenses, lesson 3 communicative phrases and listening. Preposition expressions are a standing homework from RUB7 onward.
- **Muurame RUB7 (S9)** lists four structures for the last compulsory B1 course: adjective inflection and use, word order, s-passive, past participle as adjective.

### 2.3 What students are told about the writing section [V]
- Polvijärvi: write a text every couple of weeks during study leave, or "pisteet voivat pudota roimasti". Learn the formalities of each genre (email, letter, description, speech, opinion piece, blog), and remember the three things that score: "sisältö, rakenne ja sanasto" (S1).
- Töölö: write the requested length "vaikka väkisin", because "Tyhjästä ei saa pisteitä". Plan on scrap paper as a mind map, and pick the prompt you can write more about *in Swedish* (S11, S11b). Fabritius adds that RB marking is about communication: "tärkeintä on, että lukija ymmärtää sinua", and RB does not weigh cohesion or idiomaticity the way the long syllabus does. This matches the three RB criteria in `ytl-exam-format-hvp.md` §3.1 [V there]. Polvijärvi's copy of the YTL criteria does put "idiomaattisesti" inside the range-and-accuracy criterion (S2's sibling page …/ruotsi/yo-koe).
- Jukarainen: write directly in Swedish rather than translating from Finnish. Vary straight and inverted word order. Avoid worn phrases and passives you can't handle (S18).

## 3. Rules and mnemonics teachers use, and where they are wrong or oversimplified

### 3.1 Mnemonics students will already know [V]
| Mnemonic / phrasing | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **KON-SU-KIE-PRE** (konjunktio, subjekti, kieltosana, predikaatti) for subordinate clauses; Papegojan writes **KON-SU-LI/KIE-PRE** (LI = *liikkuva määre*) | S2, S4, S14; also an Aalto handout (search snippet) | This is the Finnish school mnemonic. **BIFF appears in none of the Finnish teacher materials**, so our course should lead with KON-SU-KIE-PRE and mention BIFF only as the Swedish name [I]. |
| "**V2: päälauseessa taipunut verbi aina kakkospaikalla**" / "verbi toisena" | S2, S4, S11b, S14 | Töölö's warm-up tells students to check every sentence for "verbi kakkosena". |
| "**Vanha aviopari**": subject and verb always sit side by side (SU+PRE or PRE+SU), with the negation/movable adverb right after the pair | S4 | Good image. We can reuse the idea in our own words. |
| *Liikkuvat määreet* list: *inte, aldrig; sällan, ofta, alltid, ännu, redan; tyvärr, kanske, säkert; alltså, ändå, dessutom* | S14 | Clean, correct list. |
| Coordinating conjunctions *och, men, för, eller, så* (+ *utan*) sit in "0-paikka" and don't change word order | S2, S4, S14 | Correct. Important because *för* (= sillä) and *så* are often mis-analysed as subordinating. |
| **ABC cases** after determiners: A = indefinite adjective + indefinite noun (*någon, ingen, annan, sådan, vilken, varje, all/alla, många, få, numerals*); B = a-form + definite noun (*den här/där, hela, båda, själva, den/det/de*); C = a-form + indefinite noun (genitive, possessives, *denna/detta/dessa, samma*) | S8 (Muhos .docx), the same split in S2 | Matches the exam's most-tested noun-phrase errors (catalogue §5.2). |
| "***pappas nya bil***": after a possessive or genitive, the noun is indefinite and the adjective takes -a | S11 | Töölö calls it "muistisääntö" and "Tämä on sääntö!". |
| Adverb from adjective = the *t*-form ("-sti: ett-suvun muoto"): *Hon sjunger vackert* | S2 | Correct for most adjectives. |
| "Predikaattiverbi (lauseen 1. verbi) ei ole perusmuodossa"; "Apuverbin jälkeen pääverbin perusmuoto"; "Verbi yleensä aina taipuu, ellei ole syytä…" (a modal or *att* before it) | S4, S11 | Correct framing of finite vs non-finite. |
| *vara/bli* + profession/nationality/belief → no article, but add *en/ett* when there is an adjective or relative clause (*bli en bra fotbollsspelare*) | S4, S14 | Correct. Exam-relevant (catalogue noun forms). |
| Finnish partitive (-a/-ä) ≈ Swedish noun with no article (*Ge mig smör*, *Det finns bilar*); Finnish essive -na ≈ *som* + bare noun (*som barn*) | S4, S14 | Good contrastive rules. |
| "Ruotsissa ei ole vill-futuuria": future = present + time word / *ska* / *kommer att*; *vill* = haluta | S4, S11 | Correct, and targets English interference. |
| *för … sedan* = sitten ("vuosi sitten = FÖR ett år sedan") | S2, S13 | Correct. |

### 3.2 Phrasings that are wrong or oversimplified (avoid copying)
| # | Teacher text | Source | Problem | What we should say [I] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Laita subjektit määräiseen muotoon silloin, kun subjektina on SUBSTANTIIVI YKSIN tai … ADJEKTIIVI + SUBSTANTIIVI." | S4 | **Wrong as a general rule.** Indefinite subjects are normal: *En kvinna ringde*, *Barn behöver sömn*, *Ny forskning visar…*, and after *det finns*. | Definiteness depends on whether the referent is known or generic (Papegojan's rules in S14 are correct), not on subject position. |
| 2 | "Laita prepositioiden jälkeen määräinen muoto ellei kyseessä ole sanonta." | S4 | **Wrong as a general rule.** *i ett stort hus, med en vän, på en restaurang, utan pengar, till Sverige*. | Preposition phrases follow the same known/new logic. Some fixed phrases have no article (*på landet, i stan, till sängs, med bil*). |
| 3 | "Jag är säker att han kommer med." | S13 (Otava) | Non-standard. The standard construction is ***säker på att***, which S4 and S11b both teach correctly. | Teach only *säker på att* / *Det är säkert att*. |
| 4 | "*ska* merkitsee ensisijaisesti 'pitää tehdä jtkn'" | S13 | Oversimplified. *ska* is primarily plan, intention or arrangement (*Jag ska resa till Oslo* = aion/olen lähdössä), with obligation as a second meaning. The catalogue shows *ska* scored 0 p in *Jag glömmer aldrig*. | *ska* = plan/promise/arrangement or obligation; *kommer att* = prediction; present + time word for schedules. |
| 5 | "En av de mest positiva sidor hos dig…" (answer key) | S11b (prep FACIT) | **Grammar error in the key.** It should be *sidorna*: *en av de* + superlative + **definite plural**. The exam tests exactly this (*en av de främsta orsakerna*, catalogue §4.1 noun forms). | Make "en av de + superlative + -na" an explicit item. |
| 6 | "Konstgjord kött är dyrt." | S13 | Agreement error: *kött* is an ett-word, so ***konstgjort*** *kött*. | Copy-editing reminder: check every example. |
| 7 | "Kan du låna mig pengar?" (cue: *voitko lainata minulle rahaa*) | S11b (vocab sheet) | Colloquial. Standard Swedish is ***låna ut*** (pengar till mig), and *låna* = borrow (*Kan jag låna pengar av dig?*). | Teach *låna* (borrow) / *låna ut* (lend). Exam keys follow the standard language. |
| 8 | "allt (= kaikki asioista) vai alla (= kaikki ihmisistä)" | S4 | Oversimplified. *alla* is plural "all/everyone" for things too (*alla böcker*). *allt* = everything / all + mass noun (*allt vatten*), and *all* + en-mass noun (*all mat*). | Use a number/countability rule, not a people-vs-things rule. |
| 9 | "Päälauseissa predikaatti on aina toisena." | S2 (also S14 "aina kakkospaikalla") | True for statements only. Questions (*Kommer du?*) and imperatives put the verb first. Our own unit review flagged the same wording (units-review-a). | Say "väitelauseessa" (declarative main clause). |
| 10 | "För ei vastaa kysymykseen kuinka kauan vaan miksi ajaksi: *Vi åkte till England för en vecka*." | S4 | Acceptable, but risky to teach. Natives mostly say *i en vecka* / *en vecka* for duration, and *för en vecka* can be mixed up with *för en vecka sedan*. The oral list in S5 has "Jään kahdeksi viikoksi" as a target. | Teach *i två veckor* / *två veckor* for duration and *på två veckor* for "within". Accept *för två veckor* for intended duration, but don't lead with it. |
| 11 | "Ja / Jo = kyllä" | S16 | Oversimplified. *jo* answers a *negative* question (*Kommer du inte? – Jo!*). | One-line note. |
| 12 | Writing samples use *dej/mej* (*Sköt om dej!*, *Hör av dej!*) | S7b (Fokus 3), S4 | Not wrong (an accepted colloquial spelling), but exam keys print *dig*. | Teach *dig/mig*. Mention *dej* only as an informal variant. |
| 13 | "Sivulauseessa on AINA suora sanajärjestys" | S14 | Correct for exam purposes. Edge cases (main-clause order in *att*-clauses in speech, conditional inversion without *om*) are not RB material. | Keep the rule, maybe with a footnote for strong students. |
| 14 | ABC lists put *all/alla* in group A (indefinite noun) | S8 | Incomplete. *alla barnen* (all the children) is also correct, with a meaning difference. *hela* + definite (*hela dagen*) is correctly in B. | Note *alla barn* vs *alla barnen*. |

Minor typos in sources (*år* for *är* in S2's example; *Kan ja få* in S13) don't matter, but they are a reminder to proofread our own examples.

## 6. Exercise formats teachers use for abi revision [V]
1. **Old yo-exams as the main material.** Töölö RUB18(8) works through 2023 S → 2022 K → 2022 S part by part, with homework by item number (S12). Muurame does a yo-listening once a week and yo-writing at home in Google Drive (S9). Polvijärvi lists which exams were already used in class (S1). Prelims are scored with real grade boundaries, plus feedback (S11).
2. **Short Finnish → Swedish translation sentences** ("Käännöslauseita", 25 items: *Minun uusi autoni*, *Minun mielestäni*, *Minä pidän lukemisesta*, *Viisi vuotta sitten*, *Huomenna minä tulen ajoissa kouluun*, *Kotona minä olen onnellinen*, *Poika, joka juoksee…*, *Syön mielelläni ravintolassa*, *Matkustan ulkomaille*). Each item targets one trap: possessive + indefinite noun, *tycka om att*, *för … sedan*, inversion, *hemma*, relative clause, *gärna*, *utomlands* (S7b).
3. **Dialogue translation "kuin käännöstehtävä"**: Töölö tells students to translate dialogue cues "mahdollisimman tarkasti" and practise with past items such as *Klädproblem* (2020 S) and *Prat om hälsan* (2021 K), checking against the official scoring (S11b).
4. **Preposition gap-fills** (verb / noun / adjective + preposition, then complete the sentence yourself: *Jag är stolt över …*) (S11b). Polvijärvi spends four lessons on this (S3).
5. **Situational rejoinder choice** while listening (pick the fitting reply) (S11b).
6. **Self-correction checklists** for written work: word order → noun forms → adjectives → genitive/pronoun forms → preposition chunks (S2, S4).
7. **Diary/blog writing**: 4 days × 50–100 words, focusing on tense choice (S11b). Weekly *kotiaineet* (S3).
8. **Grammar mind map** of everything learned, with "bonus" items (S7).
9. **Oral exam (suullinen tentti)** with metalinguistic questions, such as how the subject differs in active and passive sentences, where the *-s* goes on a multi-verb predicate, and when the agent is used (S5).
10. **Conditional sentence writing** from given Finnish verb pairs (*ostaisin – maksaisi*) (S10).
11. **Picture vocabulary labelling** plus gap sentences with Finnish cues (S11b).
12. **Word tests** after every text or theme vocabulary (Muurame: every Tuesday) (S9). Quizlet/Kahoot sets (S1, S7, S10).
13. **External self-check drills** teachers link to: Kielikompassi/Nätfräsch (JYU: passive, participles, adjectives), Papegojan (HY), HAMK preposition drills, Abitreenit (S9, S14).

## 7. Common-mistake lists ("yleisimmät virheet") [V]

### 7.1 Töölö RUB8 prelim feedback, "Usein toistuvia mutta aika helposti vältettäviä virheitä kirjoitelmissa" (S11)
1. **Text far too short.** "Kirjoita pyydetty pituus 'vaikka väkisin'!"
2. **Overusing *jätte-***, and writing it apart from the next word. Write *jättebra, jättemycket* as one word and vary with *väldigt, enormt, oerhört*.
3. **Definite noun after a possessive** (*\*min dagen, \*mina kompisarna*). Correct: *min dag, mina kompisar, pappas nya bil*.
4. **Personal pronoun forms** (subject/object/possessive) and *sin/sitt/sina* + reflexives.
5. **Typos that create non-words**: *\*läxör, \*människör, \*görä, \*hörä*. Swedish has no -ör plural.
6. ***intresserad av*** (spelling + preposition) vs *intressant*.
7. ***tid / en timme / dags för / en dag / en stund***, and *Det var länge sen!*
8. **Wrong or missing preposition.**
9. **Verb inflection and tense consistency**, and inflecting the verb after a modal or *att*.
10. **Future with *vill*** (*vill* = want).
11. ***ny* vs *nu***.
12. ***flyga / flytta / fly / flyta*** (flytande = fluent).

### 7.2 Punkalaidun "HUOMAA TÄRKEÄT EROT" and English-interference list (S4)
*kanske/ganska; svår/svart; lära/lära sig; ny/nu; allt/alla; lite/liten; betyda* (things) vs *mena* (people); *mennä* → *åka/resa/gå*; *träffa/träffas; här/där/hit/dit; pitäisi = borde; timme/tid/stund; Vi vill ha roligt; säker/säkert/saker; Jag är säker på att… / Det är säkert att…; Det är \*hård att studera → jobbigt*. English traps: *några* (some) vs *som*; *vill* ≠ will; *\*mycket av* (lots of) → *massor av / mycket pengar*, but *många bilar*; *there is* → *det finns*. Spelling: å/ä/ö, capitals, end punctuation. Also *man* consistency (don't switch to *han/hon*), and only one negation per clause.

### 7.3 Teacher interview (S18)
- The most common error is **perfect tense formed wrongly**: *ha* is right but the main verb is put in the past tense instead of the supine ("perfekti muodostetaan väärin").
- **Noun forms after pronouns** (*någon bil, den här bilen, min bil*) separate the higher grades.
- **Plural pronouns**: "Kaikkein tavallisin virhe on, että monikon toinen persoona *ni* sekoitetaan muotoon *de*."

### 7.4 Polvijärvi "Muista" box (S2)
*aikoa = tänka / ska; ajatella jtk = tänka på; olla jotain mieltä = tycka; pitää jostakin = tycka om; mennä jalan = gå, kulkuneuvolla = åka/resa/fara; vuosi sitten = för ett år sedan*.

## 8. Agreement and contradictions with the exam catalogue [I]

**Where teachers and exams agree**
- Word order is the top teacher topic (12/14) and the only grammar topic in all 19 RB sittings (catalogue §4.1 #1). Teachers drill V2 and KON-SU-KIE-PRE, which is exactly what the three-permutation items test.
- Prepositions, adjective inflection and noun definiteness come next for teachers (10–11/14). The exam has them at #2, #3 and #5. Determiner-triggered forms (ABC / *pappas nya bil*) are taught by 8/14 sources and match the exam's *Floras bästa råd*, *största delen*, *vårt naturliga sömnmönster* items.
- Teachers' error lists (*säker på*, *intresserad av*, *för … sedan*, *hit/här*, *hem/hemma*, *vill ≠ will*, *det finns*) overlap with the catalogue's Finnish-learner errors (§5).

**Where teachers under-weight what the exam now tests**
- ***för att / genom att / utan att* + infinitive**: only 3/14 teacher sources name it, and none drills it as a contrast set. In the exam it went from 1 RB gap (2017–21) to 7 (2022–26), and connectors overall are exam unit #4. **This is a gap our course can fill.**
- **Connective adverbs and adverb vocabulary** (*samtidigt, tvärtom, dessutom, troligen, egentligen, alltmer*): almost absent from teacher grammar lists (only Papegojan's movable-adverb list and Muurame's adverb Quizlet), yet adverbs rank #7 and connective adverbs are rising.
- **Verb vocabulary in context** (the single largest exam tag, 44 gaps) shows up only as theme word tests and the Otava lookalike lists. Nobody trains choosing among three same-form verbs with a shared prefix (*förklarar/fördelar/förevisar*).
- **Phrase banks and guided replies** (the 2025–26 formats) don't appear in any teacher material I found. Teachers still prepare the 5 × 3 dialogue translation.

**Where teachers over-weight relative to RB exams**
- **s-passive** (5/14), **imperative** (3/14), **conditional** (5/14) and **participles** (4/14) get whole lessons. In RB the passive is falling (6 → 3 gaps) and imperative and conditional are rare (they show up in translation cues such as *Gå längs…* and *Det skulle hjälpa mig*). Polvijärvi brackets passive and participles as optional, which matches the exam data.
- **Numerals** (3/14) appear in old teacher material but not in the RB catalogue.

**No direct contradictions on facts.** Teachers' exam-format information (299 p; 200–350 / 400–600 characters; −3/−6 for going over; three criteria) matches `ytl-exam-format-hvp.md` (S1's yo-koe page, S11b). The one nuance: Töölö says RB writing doesn't weigh cohesion and idiomaticity "kuten pitkän kielen kirjoitelmissa", while the YTL criterion text that Polvijärvi quotes still asks how "idiomaattisesti" vocabulary is used. Both are compatible with the communicativeness-driven RB scale.

## Implications for our course content

1. **Use the students' own mnemonics.** Teach word order as V2 ("verbi toisena väitelauseessa") plus **KON-SU-KIE-PRE** (with LI/movable adverbs from Papegojan's list), the "subject and verb always together" image, and "*och/men/för/eller/så* sit outside the clause". Give BIFF only as a footnote. Fix the wording to "väitelauseessa" so questions and imperatives aren't contradicted.
2. **Build one "noun phrase" unit around the ABC split** (determiner → adjective form → noun form), with *pappas nya bil* as the anchor. Add the items teachers miss: ***en av de* + superlative + definite plural** (*en av de största orsakerna*), *största delen*, *hela dagen*, *alla barn / alla barnen*, *egen* after possessives (*mitt eget rum*). Include an explicit "definiteness is about known vs new, not subject position or prepositions" rule, so students unlearn the S4 oversimplifications.
3. **Give *för att / genom att / utan att / efter att ha*** its own drill set with Finnish cues (*-kseen / -malla / -matta / -ttuani*). The exam rewards it more and more, and teachers barely drill it, so this is our differentiator.
4. **Add a "connective adverbs and same-prefix verbs" vocabulary track** in the exam's 3-option format (*samtidigt / följaktligen / däremot*; *förklarar / fördelar / förevisar*). Teacher courses don't cover this.
5. **Preposition chunks as a spaced-repetition deck**: verb + preposition, adjective + preposition and noun + preposition, merging the lists in S2 and S11b (≈60 items: *intresserad av, rädd för, säker på, stolt över, nöjd med, van vid, trött på, orsak till, brist på, exempel på, dags för, delta i, njuta av, lida av, bero på, längta efter, ägna sig åt…*). Use it in gap-fills and in writing.
6. **A "15 classic errors" checklist** for the writing module, built from §7. Cover the possessive + definite noun error, the perfect formed as *har* + past, *ni/de*, *vill* as future, *säker på att*, *intresserad av*, *tid/timme/stund*, *ny/nu*, *jätte-* compounds, *det finns*, *för … sedan*, *hit/här*, finite verb after a modal, *man* consistency, and length. Show it in the AI feedback rubric and as a pre-submit checklist.
7. **Writing phrase cards in two registers** (from §4.1): informal (*Hej! … Hör av dig! Ha det så bra! Hälsningar*) and formal (*Jag ser fram emot ert svar. Med vänlig hälsning*). Add an opinion kit (*Jag anser att…, Å ena sidan… men å andra sidan…, Fördelen med… är…, För det första…, Jag håller med / inte med…*). Use standard *dig/mig* spelling.
8. **A situational-phrases unit** matching the exam's PRAG items: *Krya på dig!, Lycka till!, Vad synd!, Grattis!, Tack för senast!, För all del, Det gör inget, Förlåt att jag är sen, Ursäkta, är det ledigt här?* Run it as "pick the right rejoinder" (3 options), the format both Töölö and YTL use.
9. **Keep passive, conditional and imperative light**: short recognition lessons plus translation cues (*Det skulle hjälpa mig*, *Gå längs gatan*), not full units. That matches RB frequency and Polvijärvi's "optional" marking.
10. **Train exam formats teachers haven't caught up with**: phrase-bank gaps (2025 S – 2026 S) and guided replies from Finnish instructions (2026 S). Keep chunk-scored dialogue translation framed as "translate precisely", as Töölö advises.
11. **Add a confusables mini-deck** (§5.3): *ligga/lägga, sitta/sätta, sakna/missa, betyda/mena, tänka/tycka/tro, gå/åka, byta/ändra/förändra, bjuda/erbjuda/förbjuda, känna/känna till/veta, redan/fortfarande, önska/hoppas, träffa/träffas, låna/låna ut*. It also feeds the exam's lookalike distractors.
12. **QA rule for our own content**: none of the error patterns in §3.2 may appear in our examples or keys (*säker att*, *konstgjord kött*, *en av de … sidor*, *kan du låna mig*, blanket "definite after prepositions/for subjects"). Add them to the content-review checklist used in `units-review-*.md`.
13. **Include a list of the Swedish words used in exam instructions** (*välj det naturligaste svarsalternativet, översätt den understrukna meningen, komplettera…*). Töölö found it worth a handout, and it costs us one screen.
