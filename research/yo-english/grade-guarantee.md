# "E tai rahat takaisin" – grade guarantee research (EA, pitkä englanti)

Researched 30.10.2026. Sources were fetched on that date; competitor terms change every season, so re-check before launch.
Labels: **[V]** verified from the cited page or official file, **[S]** secondary or partial source, **[O]** my opinion or recommendation.

Context: Opiskeluakatemia's Englannin abikurssi costs 44.99 €, runs on Stripe, and is valid until 22.3.2027, the EA exam day (see `research/abikurssi-market/REPORT.md`).

## 1. Finnish guarantees: what competitors actually offer

### 1.1 WordDive – "Enkusta E tai rahat takaisin" (the only direct grade guarantee found) [V]
Source: https://www.worddive.com/fi/kielikurssit/englannin-abikurssi/ and https://www.worddive.com/fi/takuupalautus

- **Price:** "Kurssin hinta on 99€, ja se sisältää täyden arvosanatakuun."
- **Two goals (päämäärät):** E goal, "Arvosanatakuu: vähintään E tai rahat takaisin.", and C goal, "Arvosanatakuu: vähintään C tai rahat takaisin." The two goals differ in difficulty, and the student can switch goal after starting.
- **Who is covered:** "Takuu on voimassa sekä pitkän että lyhyen oppimäärän yo-kirjoituksiin osallistuville ja aiempaa arvosanaansa korottaville." This covers both syllabi and also retakers.
- **Only usage condition:** "Suoritat vähintään yhden abikurssin päämääristä valmiiksi (100 %) viimeistään englannin yo-koetta edeltävänä päivänä." Optional material is not required: "Lisämateriaalin suorittamista ei vaadita arvosanatakuuta varten." The page does not say what "100 %" measures. It gives an average completion time of 35 h ("Abikurssin keskimääräinen suoritusaika on 35 tuntia").
- **No starting-level test**, no mock-score requirement and no activity-spread requirement.
- **Refund:** "Kurssimaksu maksetaan kokonaisuudessaan takaisin ilmoittamallesi tilille…", within one week of WordDive approving the claim.
- **Claim:** made on the takuupalautus page, which opens only when results come out. Autumn 2026 deadline: "Syksyn 2026 yo-kokeet: palautuspyynnöt 30.11.2026 mennessä." If the student files an appeal (oikaisuvaatimus), the claim is still made on the original grade within the schedule.
- **Proof:** a screenshot from Wilma or Oma Opintopolku, or a photo or scan of the grade printout from the school office. WordDive may check grades with the school and does random spot checks.
- **Exclusions:** none listed beyond the conditions above.
- **Consumer experience** (vauva.fi thread, [S]): one parent missed the claim deadline. Others note that the whole course must be completed and that results depend heavily on the student's starting level. No one in the thread clearly reports receiving a refund. https://www.vauva.fi/keskustelu/4508371/
- WordDive's Swedish course uses the same model: "Ruotsista M tai rahat takaisin". https://www.worddive.com/fi/kielikurssit/ruotsin-abikurssi/

**Takeaway [O]:** WordDive can afford this because the only gate is reaching 100 % completion of a roughly 35 h course before the exam. That gate also acts as a starting-level filter, since weak students rarely finish. The price is 99 €, and the refund is only the course fee.

### 1.2 Valmennuskeskus (Eezy) – credit and retake guarantees, no grade guarantee [V]
- **Abinetti A-englanti (215 €):** no grade guarantee. The only promise is "Mikäli haluat jatkaa kurssin käyttöä syksyn 2027 kirjoituksiin, voit ilmoittautua syksyn kurssille maksutta." The pages also make a statistical claim: "70 % abikurssimme käyneistä saavutti tavoittelemansa arvosanan ja 30 % ylitti…" https://valmennuskeskus.fi/kurssi/abinetti-a-englanti-kevat-2027/ , https://valmennuskeskus.fi/abikurssi/
- **Lääkis (pääsykoe) courses** have two guarantees. https://valmennuskeskus.fi/kurssi/laakis-vip-etaopetus-2026-2027/
  - Price guarantee: "Mikäli et saa opiskelupaikkaa lääketieteellisistä tiedekunnista, pääset vastaavalle kurssille seuraavana vuonna puoleen hintaan!" This is a 50 % retake, not money back.
  - Satisfaction guarantee: "Voit osallistua opetukseen 2 kertaa / oppiaine ja päättää sen jälkeen vastaako kurssi odotuksiasi. Mikäli et ole tyytyväinen kurssiin, palautamme maksamasi summan kokonaisuudessaan takaisin."
- **General terms:** "Takuu on kurssikohtainen…". The guarantee must be used in the next spring application round, and "Takuun voi käyttää vain saman alan vastaavalle kurssille, jolle olet aiemmin osallistunut". To claim, the student ticks a box in the shopping cart. The terms page also sets payment-method age limits: "OP Lasku edellyttää 20-vuoden ikää ja Walleyn käyttö edellyttää 18-vuoden ikää." https://valmennuskeskus.fi/tilaus-ja-toimitusehdot/

### 1.3 Lääkisvalmennus – "takuuedut" (retake discounts) [V]
- The benefits apply "jos korkeasta sisäänpääsyprosentistamme huolimatta tavoite jää saavuttamatta". Examples are a LääkisTEHO course at half price, LääkisETÄ or TREENI free, and a free study package. The April course gives 200 € off instead. There is no cash refund and no stated activity requirement. https://www.laakisvalmennus.fi/laaketieteellisen-valmennuskurssit/laaketieteellisen-teho-valmennuskurssit/

### 1.4 Others checked
- **MAFY:** I found no guarantee. The terms say digital services carry no statutory withdrawal right once performed ("Palvelutuotteilla tai digitaalisella sisällöllä ei ole … peruutusoikeutta"). A course counts as used "kun kurssin aikataulusidonnaiset osat … ovat menneet." For compulsory-education-age students, the terms only say to contact customer service. https://mafy.fi/kaupan-toimitusehdot/ [V]
- **Eximia:** eximia.fi refused the connection, so this is not verified.
- **Studeo, Preppaus, Aspira, Otava Opisto, Abitreenit (Yle, free):** searches found no grade guarantee. [S]
- **Market check** (own earlier report): Solva, Lara, Mafy and Eezy offer no grade guarantee, so WordDive is the only one (`research/abikurssi-market/REPORT.md`).

**Pattern:** Finnish pääsykoe providers guarantee *a discounted or free retake*, not money. The only cash grade guarantee in the YO-prep market is WordDive's, which is cheap, app-based and has a single completion gate.

## 3. Finnish legal frame

### 3.1 Kuluttajansuojalaki (38/1978) – verified against the current Finlex text [V] https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/1978/38
- **2:6 Misleading information:** "Markkinoinnissa tai asiakassuhteessa ei saa antaa totuudenvastaisia tai harhaanjohtavia tietoja, jos tiedot ovat omiaan johtamaan siihen, että kuluttaja tekee ostopäätöksen … jota hän ei ilman annettuja tietoja olisi tehnyt." A headline "E tai rahat takaisin" whose conditions most buyers cannot meet is the main risk here.
- **2:7 Omitting essential information:** "…ei saa jättää antamatta sellaisia asiayhteys huomioon ottaen olennaisia tietoja, jotka kuluttaja tarvitsee ostopäätöksen … tekemiseksi". Under this rule the eligibility conditions are essential information, so they must be shown where the claim is made and before purchase, not only in the terms.
- **2:2 Minors:** marketing that reaches minors is "hyvän tavan vastaista erityisesti, jos siinä käytetään hyväksi alaikäisen kokemattomuutta tai herkkäuskoisuutta…"; the assessment takes into account "alaikäisten ikä ja kehitystaso". Abiturients are 17–19, so the strictness is moderate but real.
- **2:8a** lists the information that must be given before a contract is made; **6:9 and 6:12** cover advance information in distance selling.
- **6:15 Digital content:** the 14-day withdrawal right is lost only if the consumer "on antanut nimenomaisen ennakkosuostumuksensa … ja antanut hyväksyntänsä sille, että hän näin menettää peruuttamisoikeutensa", and the seller confirms this durably under 6:13. The guarantee is separate from this statutory right and must not be presented as the same thing.
- **3:1 Unfair terms:** "Elinkeinonharjoittaja ei saa käyttää … sopimusehtoa, jota … on pidettävä kuluttajien kannalta kohtuuttomana." Hidden or impossible conditions risk falling under this.
- **4:3 Ambiguous terms:** "…ja ehdon merkityksestä syntyy epätietoisuutta, ehtoa on tulkittava kuluttajan hyväksi." Every condition therefore needs to be objectively measurable and shown to the student, for example on a progress screen.
- **Sanctions:** the Consumer Ombudsman supervises marketing (2:20), and 2:19a provides for a penalty payment (seuraamusmaksu).
- **Not verified on Finlex:** the EU unfair commercial practices blacklist (Directive 2005/29/EC Annex I, point 10) forbids presenting rights consumers already have by law as a distinctive feature of the offer. Do not market the 14-day withdrawal right as part of the guarantee.

### 3.2 KKV guidance [V unless noted]
- Warranties are voluntary but must not restrict statutory rights. KKV's Bauhaus decision: "Takuusta on käytävä ilmi selkeästi takuun sisältö ja että ostajalla on lainmukaiset oikeudet eikä takuu rajoita näitä oikeuksia." https://www.kkv.fi/paatokset/kuluttaja-asiat/kuluttaja-asiamiehen-ratkaisut/kkv-513-14-08-01-05-2015/
- KKV's 2016 consumer-scam report lists "'Rahat takaisin, jos et ole tyytyväinen' -lupaukset eivät ole toteutuneet" as a known problem. Honoring claims fast is a reputational requirement. https://www.kkv.fi/uploads/sites/2/2021/11/2016-kkv-selvityksia-1-2016-kuluttajahuijaukset.pdf [S – snippet]
- **Refund method:** "Rahat tulee palauttaa kuluttajan käyttämällä maksutavalla." Other methods are allowed only with consent, and the refund must cost the consumer nothing. https://www.kkv.fi/kuluttaja-asiat/tietoa-ja-ohjeita-yrityksille/verkkokauppiaille/maksun-palauttaminen-verkkokaupassa/ (This page is about withdrawal refunds; applying it to the guarantee is my inference.)
- **Minors and marketing:** marketing to minors is judged more strictly. The rules include no direct calls to buy aimed at children and no urging them to persuade parents. https://www.kkv.fi/en/consumer-affairs/children-as-consumers/marketing-aimed-at-children/ , https://www.kkv.fi/ratkaisut-ja-julkaisut/julkaisut/kuluttaja-asiamiehen-linjaukset/aihekohtaiset/alaikaiset-markkinointi-ja-ostokset/ [S – the full page text was not retrievable]

### 3.3 Minors as buyers – Holhoustoimilaki 442/1999 [V] https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/1999/442
- **24 §:** "Vajaavaltainen voi tehdä oikeustoimia, jotka ovat olosuhteisiin nähden tavanomaisia ja merkitykseltään vähäisiä." The next section lets a minor dispose of money earned by their own work. A transaction made without the required consent becomes binding only "jos hänen edunvalvojansa tai hän itse täysivaltaiseksi tultuaan sen hyväksyy."
- A 44.99 € study course for a 17-year-old is plausibly "tavanomainen ja vähäinen". The Consumer Disputes Board has held a 12-year-old's ~70 € clothing purchase to be such ([S] Minilex https://www.minilex.fi/a/alaik%C3%A4isen-oikeus-tehd%C3%A4-oikeustoimia). The guarantee itself adds no payment obligation for the student, so it only benefits them.
- **Practical point [O]:** the refund goes to the original payment method, which is often a parent's card. Name the payer as the refund recipient, and let a guardian accept the terms at checkout if the student is under 18.

### 3.4 Payment provider (Stripe) [V] https://docs.stripe.com/refunds
- "Refunds can only be sent back to the original payment method used in a charge." Refunds to expired or cancelled cards are normally routed by the issuer. If a refund fails, it returns to the balance "up to 30 days" later and you must "arrange an alternative way to provide your customer with a refund". The terms should therefore allow a bank-transfer fallback.
- "Stripe's processing fees from the original transaction aren't returned." Each refund costs the fee, about 0.3–1 € on a 44.99 € sale.
- Claims come 2–3 months after purchase (spring results arrive in May), so card refunds will mostly be ordinary refunds, not reversals.

## 5. Recommendation

### 5.1 Design principles [O]
1. **Gate on starting level or demonstrated level, not only on effort.** With a 20–24 % base rate, an effort-only rule like WordDive's is affordable only if completion itself filters strongly. Polku is gamified to be easy to finish, so it would filter less.
2. **Every condition must be computable server-side from logged data**, shown live in the app as a "Takuu-tila" checklist with ✓/✗ per condition, and stated in plain Finnish next to the headline claim (KSL 2:6, 2:7 and 4:3).
3. **Do not require the student to already be at E in mocks.** That would make the headline promise effectively empty and a misleading-marketing risk. Require an *M-level* floor, which is plausible and honest: a student at a stable M is around the E border.
4. **Pay quickly and with simple proof.** Fast payment builds trust (KKV 2016), and at 44.99 € a dispute costs more than the refund.
5. **Run one shadow season first** (spring 2027). Compute eligibility for everyone, ask for grades voluntarily (for example for a feature or prize, not a refund), and fit the thresholds to P(E | metrics). Launch the guarantee for autumn 2027 or spring 2028. This is the strongest recommendation here.

### 5.2 Concrete rule set (draft v1) [O – thresholds are starting points to calibrate]
The student is eligible only if **all** of the following hold. All conditions are measured from server logs in Europe/Helsinki time.

| # | Condition | Proposed value | Data source |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Purchased (or guarantee activated) ≥ **K = 8 weeks** before the EA exam date of the season (K2027: by 25.1.2027) | 8 weeks | purchase timestamp |
| G2 | **Entry diagnostic** completed within 14 days of purchase: timed, one sitting, a fixed item bank mapped to YTL points | Estimated score ≥ **C boundary** (≈ 200/299, 67 %). Below that the student may still buy, but the "C-goal" or no guarantee applies (mirrors WordDive's E/C split and Princeton's baselines). | diagnostic attempt |
| G3 | **Polku:** ≥ **90 %** of EA lessons passed with mastery ≥ **80 %**, and **100 % of checkpoints** passed | 90 % / 80 % | lesson_progress |
| G4 | **Mock exams:** ≥ **3 full timed mocks**, each completed in one session within the time limit, at least 2 weeks apart, the last within the **final 21 days** before the exam | 3 mocks | mock attempts |
| G5 | **Mock level:** best of the last 2 mocks ≥ **M boundary** (≈ 236/299, 79 % of the auto-scored + AI-scored total) | M floor | mock scores |
| G6 | **Essays:** ≥ **6 essays** of required length (≥ 80 % of the task's minimum characters) with AI feedback viewed, ≥ **2** of them revised and resubmitted | 6 / 2 | essay submissions |
| G7 | **Spread:** active (≥ 15 min of graded practice) on ≥ **D = 30 distinct days** across ≥ **W = 8 distinct ISO weeks** before the exam; total graded time ≥ **25 h** | 30 d / 8 wk / 25 h | session logs |
| G8 | **Vocabulary (optional):** ≥ 1 000 flashcard reviews | — | SRS log |
| G9 | Sat **EA (pitkä englanti)** in the season the course was bought for, and the YTL grade is **M or lower** (incl. I). Not covered: no-shows, an exam annulled for cheating (vilppi), or taking the short syllabus instead. | — | proof |
| G10 | Claim within **30 days** of YTL publishing the results, with a screenshot of the EA result from the YTL kokelaan asiointipalvelu or Oma Opintopolku, or the school's grade printout. Spot checks with the school. If an appeal is pending, the claim is filed on the original grade, as WordDive does. | 30 d | claim form |
| G11 | One guarantee per person and season. Account in the student's own name. First course purchase for EA (Princeton's "first program" rule). | — | account |

- **Remedy:** a full refund of the price paid, to the original payment method within 14 days of approval, with a bank-transfer fallback if the refund fails. Alternative offered alongside, student's choice: free access for the next season, as Kaplan and Valmennuskeskus do.
- **Absence for illness:** no refund. Free transfer to the next season (Valmennuskeskus model).

### 5.3 Risk considerations [O]
- **Starting level and adverse selection.** Students who expect to miss E are the most attracted to a refund promise. G2 and G5 cap the payout pool. Without them, expected payout ≈ 76–80 % × completion rate × price. With them, the eligible group sits near or above the E border, and the miss rate should be maybe 30–50 %, but this is unknown until shadow data exists. At 44.99 € even a 40 % miss rate among, say, 15 % of buyers who qualify costs about 2.7 % of revenue. It is affordable; the real risk is reputation and legal exposure, not money.
- **Deliberate underperformance** is very unlikely. The EA grade drives todistusvalinta admission points, worth far more than 45 €.
- **Gaming the completion rules:**
  - *Answer copying and shared keys:* grade server-side, draw items randomly from banks, shuffle option order per attempt, and count mastery only from first attempts or attempts with fresh items.
  - *Speed-clicking:* minimum time per item; drop sessions with implausible time on task from G3 and G7; streak days need ≥ 15 min of *graded* work, not app opens.
  - *Someone else doing the work* (a sibling with an L): hard to stop fully. G5 still requires mocks at M level. Flag abrupt changes in device or typing patterns between the diagnostic, mocks and essays. Reserve the right to void work showing "appearance of completion", quoting Princeton's clause, but list objective indicators so the clause is not kohtuuton (KSL 3:1).
  - *AI-written or copied essays:* a similarity check against model answers, other users' essays and the student's own earlier writing. An essay flagged as non-original does not count toward G6.
  - *Forged proof:* screenshots are forgeable, so keep a spot-check right and ask for the school's printout in doubtful cases. The digital certificate authenticity check (https://www.ylioppilastutkinto.fi, "Digitaalisen ylioppilastodistuksen aitouden tarkistus") only exists once the full tutkinto is complete, so it cannot be the only route.
- **Moving goalposts:** content updates must not make a student who was eligible ineligible. Snapshot the rule set version at purchase.
- **Marketing copy:** put the conditions next to the headline, e.g. "E tai rahat takaisin* – *kun suoritat Polun, 3 harjoituskoetta ja 6 kirjoitelmaa ajoissa ja lähtötasotesti ylittää C-tason. Ehdot ›". Do not use "taattu E" without conditions. Do not present the 14-day withdrawal right as a guarantee feature. Address marketing to the student but let a guardian approve the purchase if the student is under 18.
- **Legal review:** have a lawyer or the KKV business advice service read the final terms and the claim flow before launch. This document is not legal advice.

### 5.4 What to log now so the shadow season is possible [O]
Purchase time; diagnostic score; per-lesson mastery on the first attempt; per-item time; mock scores with timing and section breakdown; essay length, revision and similarity flag; daily graded minutes; device id hash. Also add an opt-in post-exam survey: "Mikä oli EA-arvosanasi?", with screenshot upload, to fit P(E | G2, G3, G5, G7).
