## 1. Exam structure (current digital exam)

### 1.1 Sections and points [V]
MÄÄR §3 and §5 and AINE list four skill areas: *kuullun ymmärtäminen* (listening comprehension), *luetun ymmärtäminen* (reading comprehension), *kirjallinen tuottaminen* (writing) and *sanaston ja rakenteiden hallinta* (vocabulary and structures). Tasks that test the same skill form one *tehtäväkokonaisuus* (task set). Tasks in a set may be placed apart in the exam, and tasks that combine skills are allowed.

For languages that include listening, which English does, the maximum is **299 points**, split as follows (MÄÄR §5; AINE):

| Section (YTL term / term used in HVPs) | Points |
|---|---|
| Kuullun ymmärtäminen / Part 1: Listening Comprehension | **80–90** |
| Luetun ymmärtäminen / Part 2: Reading Comprehension | **70–90** |
| Sanasto ja rakenteet / Part 3: Grammar and Vocabulary | **20–40** |
| Kirjallinen tuottaminen / Part 4: Production | **99** |
| **Total** | **299** |

### 1.2 Time, order and tools [V]
- The whole language exam takes place on one day and lasts **at most 6 hours** (MÄÄR §4; YLEISET 2.3.6). Work may start at 09:00 at the earliest, and candidates may not leave before 12:00 (YLEISET 2.3.6). Two short-syllabus language exams on the same day get 8 h, which does not apply to EA.
- Candidates choose the order of the tasks. They can return to the material of the written tasks, and to *some* listening material, while time remains. All answers can be changed until the candidate ends the exam (MÄÄR §4).
- The exam runs in a closed network. **No dictionaries**, paper or electronic (only Latin is an exception). Headphones connected to the computer are required. Drafting on paper is allowed, but drafts are not graded (MÄÄR §3–4).
- Listening: "Osa teksteistä voidaan kuulla useampaan kertaan, osa vain kerran", meaning some texts may be heard several times and some only once, and some listening texts may be videos (MÄÄR §5.3). HVP task instructions show the variation in practice: "You can play the video as many times as you want", "You will hear it twice", "listen once all the way through, and then listen again to each section separately".
- Task types are **not announced in advance**, and spring and autumn exams may differ in form. Possible types include "erilaiset monivalinta- ja aukkotehtävät sekä vapaat tuottamistehtävät" (various multiple-choice and gap-fill tasks and free production tasks) (MÄÄR §1.4, §3).
- Target CEFR level for English A syllabus (LOPS 2019): **B2.1** in interaction, interpreting texts and producing texts (MÄÄR §2 table).

### 1.3 Digital history [V/U]
- First digital exams: autumn 2016. Spring 2019 was the first fully paperless sitting (YTL history page: https://www.ylioppilastutkinto.fi/fi/tietopalvelut/tietoa-ylioppilastutkinnosta/ylioppilastutkinnon-historia) [V].
- English went digital in **spring 2018** [U, secondary]. Yle News (21.3.2018, https://yle.fi/a/3-10126304) says "The English language examinations were held in digital format for the first time this year". The autumn 2018 EA HVP is already a single digital-style document with Parts I–III.

### 1.4 Announced changes [V]
- **From autumn 2028, English exams (EA and EC) will be entirely in English.** No more questions or answers in Finnish or Swedish. Open tasks that currently require a Finnish answer, or give a Finnish clue, "korvataan muilla tehtävätyypeillä" (will be replaced with other task types). The reason is that the matriculation exam can be taken in English from autumn 2028. Score boundaries for English will be set using only candidates taught in Finnish or Swedish. YTL news 4.2.2025: https://www.ylioppilastutkinto.fi/fi/ajankohtaista/englannin-ylioppilaskokeet-ovat-taysin-englanninkielisia-syksyn-2028-tutkinnosta
  - Implication **[D]**: the Grammar and Vocabulary gap tasks with **Finnish clues** (for example "tunteellinen" → *emotional*) and the Finnish-answer listening and reading questions both end with autumn 2028.
- **Oral component in preparation.** It is planned to be introduced in stages in long and medium syllabus exams. Exam length and the 299 maximum stay the same, and the oral share of points will be "pieni" (small). YTL is drafting a report by the end of 2026 and will hold a consultation in early 2027. No schedule has been decided. Page updated 2.10.2026: https://www.ylioppilastutkinto.fi/fi/tutkinnon-toimeenpano/suullinen-kielitaito-ylioppilaskokeissa
- Grade-to-CEFR description (5.5.2026): an EA grade of **M (magna cum laude) or higher ≈ at least CEFR B2** ("vastaa kielitaito todennäköisesti vähintään tasoa B2"), valid for exams from spring 2012 on. https://www.ylioppilastutkinto.fi/fi/ajankohtaista/lautakunta-laatinut-kuvauksen-ylioppilaskokeiden-arvosanojen-ja-evk-taitotasojen

## 2. Task types by section (from EA HVPs 2022–2026) [V unless marked]

General scoring rules printed at the top of every recent EA HVP:
- "Täydet pisteet voi saada vain vastaus, joka ei sisällä väärää tietoa." (Only an answer that contains no wrong information can get full points.)
- "Jos avoimen kysymyksen vastauksen pituudelle on annettu merkkimäärärajoitus, seuraa sen ylittämisestä pistevähennys. **Vähennys on 1 piste** riippumatta kysymyksen maksimipisteistä." (Going over an open answer's character limit costs **1 point**, whatever the item's maximum.) MÄÄR §5.3.2 says the overrun deduction is proportional to the item maximum but at least 1 point, with the amount stated in the HVP. The EA HVPs set it at 1 point.
- Character counts exclude spaces and line breaks. The exam system counts characters but does not stop you going over (MÄÄR §5.3).

### Part 1: Listening (6–8 tasks)
- **Multiple choice** questions in English, usually **3 p per item** (for example K2026 task 2: 5 × 3 p = 15 p).
- **Open answers in Finnish** ("Vastaa suomeksi"), usually **3 p each, all or nothing (3/0 p)**, with a character cap. The cap was 150 characters (K2022–K2023), 100 (S2023 onward) and 50 or 100 (2024–2026). Each HVP lists accepted (OK), not accepted (EI) and "EI TUO EI VIE" answers. "EI TUO EI VIE" means the extra content neither gains nor loses points.
- **Spoken-interaction "most polite / most likely response" tasks**, for example "Doctor's Appointment" S2022, "Returning a Gift" K2023, "Judging a Book by its Cover" S2024, "Movie Invite" S2025 and "A Countryside Break" S2026. The candidate picks the most polite or most appropriate reply.
- **Summary gap-fill with one English word** (for example K2026 "Our Lives in the North": "Fill in each gap in the summary with only one word in English").
- Video tasks appear every season.

### Part 2: Reading (5–6 tasks)
- Multiple choice (3 p per item is typical), Finnish open answers (3/0 p), and English one-word summary gaps (for example S2025 "Stonehenge", S2026 "Little Blue Dot").
- **"Choose the best sentence / group of words for each gap"** text-completion tasks, often the largest reading task (24–30 p), for example "De-extinction" 30 p (K2024), "Doggerland" 30 p (S2024) and "Irish: Decline and Revival" 30 p (S2026).
- Other types seen: matching (for example "Logical fallacies" K2026), tables or graphs ("News Users" K2024), fiction excerpts.

### Part 3: Grammar and Vocabulary (2–4 tasks, 27–38 p in 2022–2026)
These are the recurring task types, quoting the instructions in the HVPs:
1. **Multiple-choice cloze** ("Read the text and choose the best alternative for each gap" / "…from the drop-down menu"). Usually **1 p per gap**; some sets are 2 p per gap (K2023 "Vertical Farming": "Each gap needs a different alternative. There is one extra alternative in each set"). S2025 included a "--- (nothing)" option, so leaving a gap empty was a valid choice.
2. **Productive (open) gap-fill with clues**: "Read the text carefully and fill in each gap in English according to the clue and using the correct form in each sentence. Clues can be English words to be used in the correct form or Finnish words to be translated into English. If there is a grammatical term, a suitable grammatical item should be inserted." Example clue types: an English verb to inflect (*strike* → *was struck*), a Finnish word to translate (*tunteellinen* → *emotional*), a grammatical term (*preposition*, *prepositio*), or "(No clue)". Items are 1 p, sometimes 2 p for multi-word items such as *toistensa kanssa* → *with one another / with each other*.
3. **Synonym in context**: "Which word(s) could replace the underlined word(s) in the text? Choose the alternative that best fits the context and conveys a similar meaning." Often re-uses the reading text (S2023, S2024, K2025, S2025, S2026).
4. **Error correction** (K2025 "I stand corrected", S2026 "Independence Day"): "Each sentence below has one erroneous word. The word is either misspelled or there is something wrong with the grammar… Write only the corrected word… must contain only one word." 1 p each.
5. **Register transformation** (S2023 "Cover letter"): "Replace the underlined expressions with more formal ones." 1 p each.

**How the HVPs grade productive grammar and vocabulary items:**
- Each item lists the accepted answers (often many: K2026 14.14 *soar* → *have soared / soar / are soaring / can soar / have been soaring*).
- Almost every HVP ends the open-gap task with **"Myös muut oikeat ilmaisut hyväksytään."** (Other correct expressions are also accepted.) This appears in 9 of the 10 HVPs from 2022 to 2026.
- Items are all or nothing: the point value is listed beside each accepted answer.
- I found no general statement on partial credit for spelling in grammar and vocabulary gaps. The listed answers imply that an exact correct form is needed **[D/U]**.

### Part 4: Production (writing): 1 task out of 4 prompts, 99 p
- **One** long text. Every EA exam from K2022 to S2026 offered **4 prompts** [D, verified from the HVPs].
- Length for EA: **700–1,300 characters**, excluding spaces and line breaks (MÄÄR §5.3.1.1: "Pitkän englannin ja pitkän suomen kielen kokeessa … 700–1 300 merkkiä"). Other long languages use 700–1,100 characters. HVP wording since S2023: "Please note that the recommended minimum number of characters is 700. If you write less, it is very difficult to fulfill task requirements."
- The prompts are given in English. The title and the guidance in the prompt are binding unless stated otherwise (MÄÄR §5.3.1.1).
- Penalties (MÄÄR §5.3.2.1):
  - **Over 1,300 characters: −9 p**, from the first character over, regardless of how far over.
  - **Under 700 characters: no separate deduction**, but such an answer "ei … voi yltää korkeimpiin pisteisiin" (cannot reach the top scores).
  - Small omissions from the prompt: a deduction under 10 p, for example a missing title or missing letter conventions. HVP examples: "−2 p. jos kirjeen aloitus tai lopetus puuttuu" (−2 p if the letter's opening or closing is missing), "−5 p., jos perustelut puuttuvat" (−5 p if the reasoning is missing), and "Jos kirjoittaja ei mainitse suomalaista koulutusta, −5 p." (−5 p if the writer does not mention Finnish education).
  - Mostly copied from the exam material: final score **40–0**. Plagiarised or entirely off-topic: **0**.
  - If the candidate answers more than one prompt, the lowest-scoring answer counts.
  - Copying sentences directly from the exam texts lowers the score. Using words and structures from the material is allowed.

## 3. Writing assessment criteria (EA, autumn 2023 →) [V]

Source: MÄÄR §5.3.1.1 and §6.1, also as a PDF: https://www.ylioppilastutkinto.fi/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/arvostelutaulukko-kielet-pitka.pdf

- **Viestinnällisyys** (communicativeness, meaning how well the message gets across) weighs most. The other four criteria support an overall judgement made mainly on communicativeness.
- The five criteria, as defined in the regulations:
  1. *Viestinnällisyys*: "Mitä tehtävänannon mukaista sisältöä kokelas on halunnut ilmaista, ja kuinka hän on ilmaisussaan onnistunut?" (What content required by the prompt did the candidate want to express, and how well did they succeed in expressing it?)
  2. *Tekstin sisältö ja/tai aiheen käsittely*: "Kuinka monipuolisesti kokelas käsittelee annettua aihetta?" (How thoroughly and from how many angles does the candidate treat the given topic?)
  3. *Tekstilajin mukainen rakenne* (descriptive, narrative, instructive, argumentative and reflective texts): "Kuinka johdonmukaista ja sidosteista … teksti on?" (How logical and cohesive is the text?)
  4. *Kielellinen laajuus*: "Kuinka monipuolisesti ja idiomaattisesti kokelas käyttää sanastoa ja rakenteita?" (How varied and idiomatic is the candidate's use of vocabulary and structures?)
  5. *Oikeakielisyys ja virheiden vakavuus*: "Kuinka hyvin kokelas hallitsee sanaston ja rakenteiden käytön, ja millaisia virheitä hän mahdollisesti tekee?" (How well does the candidate control vocabulary and structures, and what kinds of errors do they make?)
- **Allowed scores (only these): 99, 94, 84, 74, 64, 54, 44, 28, 14, 0.** Short versions of the descriptors, communicativeness column, quoted from the table:
  - 99: "Viesti välittyy erittäin selkeästi, luontevasti, sujuvasti ja vivahteikkaasti" (very clear, natural, fluent and nuanced). Content very varied or original; coherent structure that fits the text type; wide idiomatic range; hardly any errors.
  - 94: "kauttaaltaan selkeästi, luontevasti ja sujuvasti" (clear, natural and fluent throughout). The table gives a descriptor only in the communicativeness column at 94.
  - 84: "selkeästi ja luontevasti" (clear and natural). Fairly varied content; clear beginning and end; fairly wide, mostly idiomatic range; some errors that do not hinder the message.
  - 74: "pääosin selkeästi ja luontevasti" (mostly clear and natural). Ordinary content; may be slightly fragmented; everyday vocabulary; some errors, a few of which may hinder.
  - 64: "paikoin epäselvästi" (unclear in places). 54: "kauttaaltaan epäselvästi" (unclear throughout). 44: "kauttaaltaan heikosti" (weak throughout). 28: major gaps in content, and the communicativeness cell is empty. 14: "Viesti ei välity juuri lainkaan" (the message hardly gets across). 0: does not get across at all, or the task was not done.
- **History** [V]:
  - Until **spring 2023** the long-language scale had 33 scores: 99, 97, 95, 92, 90, 88, 85, 82, 80, 78, 75, 72, 70, 68, 65, 62, 60, 58, 55, 52, 50, 48, 45, 42, 40, 35, 30, 25, 20, 15, 10, 5, 0.
  - From **autumn 2023** the scale has 10 scores and 5 separate criterion columns; "tekstilajin mukainen rakenne" was added as its own column.
  - YTL said the change was not meant to make grading stricter or more lenient, and that it had no significant effect on grades.
  - Sources: YTL news 8.12.2022 (https://www.ylioppilastutkinto.fi/fi/ajankohtaista/toisen-kotimaisen-kielen-ja-vieraan-kielen-pitkan-oppimaaran-kirjoitustehtavan) and the YTL blog by P. Pirttisaari, 2.2.2024 (https://www.ylioppilastutkinto.fi/fi/ajankohtaista/blogi/pitkien-kielten-kirjoitussuoritusten-uusien-arvostelukriteerien-vaikutus-yo). The blog reports that the sensor left the teacher's preliminary writing score unchanged in 76.18 % of cases in S2023, compared with 46–47 % in K2022–K2023.
- **Benchmark essays ("maamerkit")** for EA, with points and sensor justifications: https://www.ylioppilastutkinto.fi/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/Englanti%20pitk%C3%A4%20oppim%C3%A4%C3%A4r%C3%A4.pdf. The blog post that links them (30.6.2023, updated 30.1.2025) is at https://www.ylioppilastutkinto.fi/fi/ajankohtaista/blogi/maamerkkeja-arvostelun-tueksi-toisen-kotimaisen-kielen-ja-joidenkin-vieraiden. I did not read the PDF's contents.
- Grading process [V]: a teacher does the preliminary grading and YTL sensors do the final grading. Teachers must use only the points listed in the HVP for productive items and only the scores in the criteria table for writing (MÄÄR §5.1).
- Multiple-choice answers: YTL may accept several options after item analysis, and the final accepted answers are published with the results (MÄÄR §4.1).

- **Boundaries are not fixed.** Since 2020 the EA "A" (pass) boundary has ranged from 126 to 153, and "L" from 263 to 278. Never present a single "pass mark" as fixed.
- How boundaries are set [V, PR]: grading is criterion-based, then the boundaries are set relatively with the **SYK method**, using only first-time candidates finishing the upper-secondary syllabus ("varsinaiset kokelaat"). The target population distribution is **L 5 %, E 15 %, M 20 %, C 20 %, B 20 %, A 15 %, I 5 %**. These shares are applied to the population's ranking by z-score average across all their exams, so the actual EA shares differ from them. Grade points for the certificate are L 7, E 6, M 5, C 4, B 3, A 2, I 0 (EVK news 5.5.2026).

What the HVPs say, in summary:
- **Structure:** Part 1 Listening, Part 2 Reading, Part 3 Grammar and Vocabulary, Part 4 Production. Every one of them totals "Total points for the exam 299 p." From 2020 on, the section totals sit inside the regulation bands (table 1.1).
- **Grammar and vocabulary:** answer keys for multiple-choice cloze. For productive gaps there are long lists of accepted forms plus "Myös muut oikeat ilmaisut hyväksytään". The clue types are English base forms, Finnish words to translate, grammatical terms ("preposition", "prepositio"), or no clue. Items are 1 p, sometimes 2 p. Older HVPs (2019) used "1/0 p" for grammar and vocabulary items and "2/0" or "3/0" for listening and reading items (K2019 scoring table).
- **Listening and reading open answers:** Finnish model answers with OK, EI and "EI TUO EI VIE" lists, all-or-nothing 3/0 p, and −1 p for exceeding the character cap.
- **Writing:**
  - The HVPs list the four prompts.
  - They add prompt-specific notes ("HUOM!") on acceptable interpretations (for example "Jos kirjoittaa kirjeen tai puheen, ei vähennystä", meaning writing a letter or a speech is not penalised) and fixed deductions for missing required elements (−2 p or −5 p).
  - For the criteria they refer to the table in MÄÄR. They tell graders "Virheet/ongelmakohdat merkitään aina" (errors and problem spots are always marked).

## 6. Conflicts and cautions

1. **Overrun deduction for open answers:** MÄÄR §5.3.2 says it is proportional to the item maximum, at least 1 p. Every EA HVP says a flat 1 p. Treat 1 p as the EA practice, since the regulations defer to the HVP.
2. **Section bands vs older exams:** S2018 to S2019 had reading at 91–101 p and grammar and vocabulary at 19–27 p, outside today's 70–90 / 20–40 bands. Don't present the bands as applying to old past papers.
3. **Writing length rules, secondary vs official:** a search snippet from a 2018 teachers' grading recommendation (peda.net, "yo-enakevat2018.pdf"; could not open it, so **[U]**) mentions tolerance for being 10 % under the limit and a deduction only at 25 % over. **Current official rule:** −9 p from the first character over 1,300, and no separate deduction for being under 700. Do not use the old rule.
4. **The 700–1,100 figure** that appears in some secondary sources is the general long-language limit. **English (and Finnish as second national language, long syllabus) is 700–1,300** (MÄÄR §5.3.1.1).
5. **The writing scale changed in autumn 2023**: from 33 scores (99, 97, 95…) to 10 (99, 94, 84, 74, 64, 54, 44, 28, 14, 0). Prep material older than 2023 may show the old scale.
6. **Finnish-language items end in autumn 2028** (all-English English exams). Content built around Finnish-answer questions or Finnish-clue gaps has a known expiry date.
7. **Date English went digital** (spring 2018) comes from a secondary source (Yle) **[U]**. I found no subject-by-subject official timeline.
8. **Not read/verified:** the maamerkit PDF contents, the paper-era HVPs from 2013 to 2018, Abitreenit and publisher materials (MOT, Otava, SanomaPro). Nothing above relies on them.
